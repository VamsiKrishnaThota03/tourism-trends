CSV format

The first row must contain these column names:

  • Year – e.g. 2023
  • Country – destination country
  • Region – geographical region
  • Visitor_Count – whole number
  • Visitor_Type – e.g. Business, Leisure
  • Season – e.g. Spring
  • Age_Group – e.g. 18-24

Example:

Year,Country,Region,Visitor_Count,Visitor_Type,Season,Age_Group
2023,France,Europe,1500000,Business,Spring,18-24
